NEET Compass is a free, non-commercial guidance website for NEET-UG students in India, at {{ settings.public_app_url }}. {% if contact %}Questions or requests: {{ contact }}.{% endif %}

{{ h("What we collect, and why") }}
  • Your account: your email or mobile number, your name, and whether you are under 18. If you sign in with Google we receive only your name, email address and Google account ID. If you sign in with your phone we receive your verified number and an account ID from our phone-verification provider.
  • For students under 18: a parent or guardian's email, and their consent (name, relationship, time). Until they consent we store none of the student's profile, ranks, scores or chats.
  • What you choose to add: class, state and domicile, preferences and budget, NEET rank or estimate, mock-test scores, practice-test answers, study plan and syllabus progress, saved colleges, draft preference lists and questions you ask the assistant.
  • Category and disability (PwD) status are optional. They are used only to compare you with the matching counselling cutoffs and are never shown to anyone else.
  • Sign-in history: the time, how you signed in, your browser and a shortened network address (the last part removed), kept for {{ settings.login_history_days }} days to keep accounts secure.
  • Server logs: page addresses, status and timing, for running the service. We do not log the contents of forms.
{{ h("What we do not do") }}
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising. There are no ads, analytics trackers or third-party cookies.
  • We do not show your marks, rank, category or scores to other students or the public.
  • Predictions come from past official cutoffs, not from profiling you.
{{ h("Who at NEET Compass can see your account") }}

A small number of site administrators can see an account's activity to run and improve the service, help students and keep accounts safe: your name and contact, how and when you sign in, your practice-test results, study-plan and syllabus progress, how many assistant chats you started and which colleges you saved.

  • Administrators cannot see your assistant chat messages, your category or your disability (PwD) status, and see only whether you added a NEET rank, not the rank itself.
  • Every time an administrator opens a student's file, it is recorded in an audit log.
{{ h("Services that process data for us") }}
  • Google, if you choose "Continue with Google": to confirm who you are.
  • Tekurious AI-Auth, if you sign in with your phone: to send and check the SMS code.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider (Anthropic Claude), when you use the assistant: your question and the facts needed to answer it, which can include your own profile and ranks. We ask them not to keep or train on this data.
  • Your browser's speech service (for example Google in Chrome), if you use voice input. Audio goes straight from your browser to that service; we receive only the text.
  • An email provider, to send a parent or guardian the consent link.
  • OpenStreetMap for maps. Your browser loads map tiles from it; no personal data is sent by us.

Some of these services may process data outside India.

{{ h("Cookies") }}

We use only essential cookies: one that keeps you signed in, one that protects forms against forgery, and short-lived ones during sign-in. Your browser may also remember small settings (like your compare list) on your own device.

{{ h("How long we keep it") }}

Your data stays until you delete your account. Sign-in history is deleted after {{ settings.login_history_days }} days. Accounts of under-18 students whose parent does not consent are deleted after {{ settings.unconsented_minor_retention_days }} days. Backups are overwritten within 14 days.

{{ h("Your rights") }}
  • See and download everything we hold about you: Profile → "Download my data".
  • Correct it: edit your profile at any time.
  • Delete your account and its data: Profile → "Delete my account". College reviews you wrote stay on the college page without your name.
  • Withdraw consent (for example a parent for their child) by deleting the account or contacting us.

We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. This policy is under legal review and may be updated; we will show the new version when you next sign in.
